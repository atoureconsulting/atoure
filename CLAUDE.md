# Instructions for Claude

## Professional documents (contracts, NDAs, letters, memos, proposals)

House style for every professional document produced for AToure:

- **Letterhead:** the AToure nav lockup (`01-logos/atoure/atoure-nav-lockup-on-light.png`) top left
  in the page header, with "STRICTEMENT CONFIDENTIEL – STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL" (or the document's
  own label) right-aligned, and a thin gold rule (`#C8A951`) under the header. The letterhead is the
  only branded element.
- **Everything else is classic contract style:**
  - Times New Roman throughout, 11 pt body, 1.15 line spacing, justified.
  - All text in black. No coloured text, no brand fonts, no letter-spacing.
  - Title centred, bold, in capitals. Headings bold, in capitals ("ARTICLE 1 – DÉFINITIONS").
    Clause numbers bold.
  - Footer in black, 8.5 pt: company details on the left, "Page X / Y" on the right, and an
    initials line ("Paraphes / Initials") on contracts.
  - Signature blocks with plain black lines.
- **Fill-in fields** stay plain text in square brackets (`[●]`, `[name]`). No highlighting.
- **Bilingual documents** (French/English) run side by side in two columns, French on the left,
  separated by a thin black line. The French version prevails unless stated otherwise.
- **Format:** an editable Word file (.docx), plus a PDF preview.
