# The AToure Sit-Down (working title)

A video series on African market access. You sit in a studio and talk to
camera. The picture cuts between you and footage that shows what you are
saying (event footage, stock video, AI-generated shots, simple graphics),
while your voice carries on underneath.

## How it works

You record each episode once, on camera, start to finish. The edit then
alternates:

- **On camera:** the viewer sees and hears you.
- **Voice over pictures:** the viewer keeps hearing you while the picture
  cuts to footage that matches the line. No separate voiceover recording is
  needed; your on-camera audio becomes the voiceover. Re-record a line on its
  own only if the room sound was bad.

Rule of thumb: never more than 10 to 15 seconds on one shot. Come back to
your face for opinions, promises and the call to action; cut away for facts,
places, examples and processes.

## What each episode produces

| Piece | Format | Length | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Master | 16:9 | 2 to 3 min | LinkedIn, YouTube, website |
| 2 to 3 cut-downs | 9:16 | 30 to 60 s | Instagram reels, LinkedIn |

All cuts carry burned-in captions: most people watch without sound.

## Where the pictures come from

Use the most real source available, in this order:

1. **Your own footage:** AToure events, the team at work, creators filming.
   Only with the agreement of the client, the creators and the venue.
2. **Licensed stock video:** for real, named places and generic event
   scenes. Use a library you hold a licence for (Canva's stock library with
   your Canva plan, or a free library such as Pexels), and keep the licence
   terms.
3. **AI-generated shots (Higgsfield):** only for generic or illustrative
   shots: a phone screen, an abstract crowd, a skyline that is not named.
   Never use AI to show a named real city, a real event, a client, a creator
   or any real person, and never to stand in for something AToure did.
4. **Graphics:** maps, numbers and lists made in code (Lemo-Opuscar) or with
   Design Engine, so borders, spelling and figures are exactly right. AI
   image tools get maps and text wrong.

Use figures only when they are real and you may use them. The sample
reports' figures are fictional: show them only with "SAMPLE" on screen, and
use a version without client logos unless the client has agreed.

## Filming guide

- **Camera:** 4K, landscape (16:9), 25 or 30 frames a second, the same for
  every episode. Frame from mid-chest up, centred, with some space above
  your head. A 4K frame leaves enough room to crop a 9:16 vertical cut from
  the middle.
- **Second angle (optional):** a side angle gives the editor somewhere to cut
  when you stumble. Without it, the editor can zoom in slightly instead.
- **Eyeline:** pick one style and keep it for the whole series. Either look
  into the lens (talking to the viewer), or look at someone just beside the
  camera (an interview).
- **Sound matters more than picture:** a clip-on microphone, a quiet room,
  soft furnishings, phones off. Record 30 seconds of silence in the room at
  the start (room tone) for the editor.
- **Light:** one large soft light about 45 degrees to one side, a weaker one
  on the other side, a little light on the background so you stand out from
  it.
- **Set:** calm and uncluttered, warm tones. One AToure touch in shot: the
  gold monogram as a framed print, or the brand colours in the set. No pure
  white walls.
- **Clothes:** plain, solid colours. Avoid fine stripes, small checks and
  logos.
- **Delivery:** film section by section, two or three takes each. Leave two
  seconds of silence before and after each section so the editor has clean
  cut points. Use a teleprompter app, or bullet points if you prefer to talk
  freely.

## Editing

- **Who edits:** your editor or CapCut or Premiere, or upload the footage
  and Claude can assemble the cut, captions and music through Higgsfield's
  editing workflow.
- **Captions:** Jost, cream on a dark band or with a soft shadow, key words
  in AToure Gold. Check every word against the script.
- **Name strip:** your name and title the first time you appear.
- **End card:** the AToure logo, atoureconsulting.com and @atoure.management.
- **Music:** quiet, under the voice, licensed. Pick trending audio for the
  Instagram cut-downs at the time of posting (Higgsfield's trending audio
  list).
- **Check before posting:** the cover with Design Engine and Impeccable, the
  wording against the rules in `.claude/skills/atoure-social/SKILL.md`.
  Drafts go into BrightBean for approval before anything is scheduled.
