# Hooks: the first line or first three seconds

A hook stops the scroll. Write it before the rest of the post, and test
several.

## Output

Six variants for the topic, one per formula below. Each is two lines:

- **Line 1, the opener:** a statement, not a question. Specific, surprising
  or concrete. Up to about 10 words.
- **Line 2, the turn:** contradicts, reframes or raises the stakes of line 1.
  Up to about 10 words.

Then: "Pick one and I'll write the full post."

## The six formulas

The examples are patterns to fill in, not facts. Fill the brackets only with
true, permitted details.

1. **Number first.** A precise figure, then the unexpected cost or method.
   > [Real number] views from one [event].
   > [The unexpected part, e.g. none of them were paid ads].
   (Only with real, permitted figures. See the rules in `SKILL.md`.)
2. **Contrarian.** State what everyone believes, then overturn it.
   > Everyone counts followers.
   > Sponsors pay for proof.
3. **Before and after.** A real change, ideally with numbers.
   > [Before: e.g. creators were a guest list].
   > [After: e.g. now they are sponsor inventory].
4. **Borrowed authority.** A known name, brand or event, then what it taught.
   > [What AToure did with a named creator, event or brand].
   > [The lesson it taught].
   (Only true events, and names AToure has the right to mention.)
5. **Admission.** A mistake or loss, then the lesson.
   > [A real mistake AToure made].
   > [What changed because of it].
6. **Future shock.** A shift that is coming, and who has not noticed.
   > Event sponsorship is being repriced.
   > Most leagues have no numbers to show.

## Checks for every hook

- Line 1 has no question mark.
- Numbers as digits (4, not four).
- No filler ("actually", "basically", "just", "so").
- No hedging ("might", "maybe", "perhaps").
- No dashes as punctuation in the hook.
- First person ("we", "I" for the founder's profile) where it fits; trend
  and authority hooks may skip it.

## By channel

- **Instagram feed and reels:** the hook is on screen in the first second
  and is also the first caption line. Keep both lines short enough to read
  on a phone without expanding the caption.
- **LinkedIn:** the hook is the first one or two lines before "see more".
  Slightly more formal; numbers and business outcomes beat emotion.
- **Website:** the hook becomes the page title and the first sentence.
