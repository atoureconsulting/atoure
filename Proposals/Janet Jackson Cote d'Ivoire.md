# Janet Jackson, Cote d'Ivoire

Confidential proposal, prepared for Rolling Stone Africa, August 2026. This is a pitch, not a delivered mandate. Positioned as "Back to the Roots."

## The concept

Michael Jackson was received by the Royal House of Sanwi in Krindjabo in 1992, integrated into the royal lineage and given the name Amalaman Anoh. He received King Nana Amon Ndoufou IV in Los Angeles in 1995, and after his death in 2009 the Kingdom held a two day royal funeral ceremony in his honour. The proposal frames a return by Janet Jackson to the same ground, more than three decades later, as the anchor for a documentary episode (Culture on Culture) and a Rolling Stone Africa LEGEND cover, both shot in Cote d'Ivoire.

## Proposed route

- **Cote d'Ivoire**: the confirmed anchor and the only leg with a fixed sequence, five days across Abidjan, Krindjabo and Yamoussoukro.
- **Potential further legs, order and dates unconfirmed**: Nigeria (Lagos), Liberia (Monrovia), Guinea (Conakry), Mauritius (Port Louis). The proposal explicitly frames these as interchangeable and open to any other African country that can offer a government partner and a workable window.

Ms Jackson's stated window of availability is the first week of November 2026, which sets the five confirmed Ivorian days. An earlier October fixture, including the Children of Africa Gala on 9 October, falls outside that window and is no longer part of the plan as written.

## Why AToure, per the proposal

- Direct institutional access into the Presidency, the Office of the First Lady, the Ministry of Tourism and the Ministry of Culture, described as relationships already activated on delivered projects rather than introductions through intermediaries.
- Security at national scale, citing the more than 600 security personnel mobilised for IShowSpeed in Ivory Coast.
- Sponsors closed in this exact market from zero pre existing relationships: MTN, Solibra and Snedai.
- Track record figures cited as proof: 1.57 billion views across the Ashton Hall Africa Tour, and the 6.1 billion digital impressions figure from the AFCON Morocco mandate (impressions should be treated as modelled per house style even where the proposal presents it as a record).

## Institutional access network named in the proposal

Presidency of the Republic; Office of the First Lady (Cabinet of First Lady Dominique Ouattara); Ministry of Tourism and Leisure (Minister Siandou Fofana); Ministry of Culture and Francophonie (Minister Françoise Remarck); Ministry of Interior and Security (Minister Gen. Vagondo Diomande); National Police, DGPN (Director General Youssouf Kouyate); municipal contacts across Greater Abidjan; aviation partners Air Cote d'Ivoire, Emirates, Jetex, Air Charter Service and AERIA; commercial partners including Canal+, Solibra, Nespresso, SOCIDA and others. The proposal is explicit that network access and collaboration potential do not constitute confirmed commitments, and every activation remains subject to formal outreach, approvals and security requirements.

## Commercial structure proposed

Two streams: AToure as ground operating partner for a fixed organisation fee, and AToure sourcing sponsorship and partnerships on a success commission basis.
