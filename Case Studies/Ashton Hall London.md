# Ashton Hall, London

Case study category: Brand Partnerships. Market: UK and international.

## The brief

Ashton Hall's team came to AToure to handle everything during his London stint: logistics, scheduling and brand opportunities. AToure moved quickly, sorted the ground work, and used its network to close a deal with a premium hospitality group while he was in town.

## What AToure delivered

- Full logistics management during Ashton Hall's London visit
- Scheduling and coordination for the full duration of the stay
- A hospitality brand deal sourced, negotiated and closed
- On the ground support from arrival to departure

## What AToure did

1. **London logistics**: managed all ground logistics during the visit, transport, scheduling and day to day coordination for Ashton Hall and his team.
2. **Brand deal**: used AToure's hospitality network to identify, approach and close a brand partnership with a premium London hospitality group.
3. **Full coverage**: end to end delivery from the first call to final wrap, so Ashton Hall could focus on the content rather than the logistics.

## Key outcomes

- Full logistics managed across the London visit
- A hospitality brand deal closed during the visit
- End to end delivery with, in AToure's own description, zero friction

No public view or reach figures are attached to this case study; it is presented on operational delivery rather than reported campaign performance.
