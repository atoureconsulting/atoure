# Spec: Visibility Report

Made by: Reporting analyst.
Saved in: `customers/<client>/<event>/`.

The product the client pays for. The full contents and data rules are in
`offers/creator-visibility-offer/report-and-data.md`. This spec covers what
the analyst produces and when.

## Two deliveries

| When | What | File |
|---|---|---|
| 48 hours after the event | Delivery check: who posted, where, against the brief, plus a chase list | `48-hour-check.md` |
| Day 14 | The full report | `report.html` and `report.pdf` |

## Inputs

- The creator brief and agreed deliverables for this event.
- Screen recordings and analytics from every creator and streamer.
- Tracked link and promo code data.
- The client account's follower count before and after.

## Done means

- Sections in the order `report-and-data.md` sets.
- Every total agrees from post log to creator table to summary.
- Every withheld field says n/a.
- Reach, impressions and media value say estimated, with the method stated.
- Missing data is listed for Baba to chase, not filled in.
- It passes `REVIEW.md`.
