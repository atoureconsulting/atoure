# Topic scoring: is it worth making?

One scale for every idea, used two ways: a quick score to rank a list, and a
deep score for a single idea before work starts.

## Seven dimensions (1 to 10 each)

Production cost and risk are reversed, so a higher score is always better.

| # | Dimension | Meaning for AToure |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reach potential | Does it hit a real pain or curiosity for organisers, sponsors, brands or creators? Is the topic being talked about? |
| 2 | Fit | Does it sit inside what AToure sells (creators, events, provable visibility) and strengthen how AToure is seen? |
| 3 | Difference | How crowded is the topic, and does AToure have an angle nobody else has (on-the-ground access, data, African markets)? |
| 4 | Shelf life | Evergreen (still useful in a year) scores high; a one-day news hook scores low |
| 5 | Business value | Can it lead to a conversation with an event, sponsor or brand, or attract creators? |
| 6 | Production cost (reversed) | 9-10: words or footage we already have. 1-3: travel, crews or many people |
| 7 | Risk (reversed) | 9-10: no client, creator or legal sensitivity. 1-3: needs permissions we don't have, or touches client confidential data |

## Scale

| Dimension | 1-3 | 4-6 | 7-8 | 9-10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reach potential | Niche, weak pain | Steady interest | Hot topic or strong pain | A pain every organiser or sponsor feels |
| Fit | Off-brand | Related but a stretch | Core topic | AToure's strongest ground |
| Difference | Saturated, no new angle | Crowded, angle possible | Moderate, clear gap | Nobody else can make it |
| Shelf life | Already stale | Useful for a week | Evergreen and timely | Evergreen |
| Business value | None | Builds credibility only | Natural lead-in to a service | Directly opens client or sponsor conversations |
| Production cost (rev.) | Crews, travel | Needs assets prepared | Simple filming or text | Ready from existing material |
| Risk (rev.) | Needs permissions we lack | Sensitive, careful wording | Minor, easy to avoid | None |

## Weights

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---|
| Reach potential | 20% |
| Fit | 20% |
| Business value | 20% |
| Difference | 15% |
| Shelf life | 10% |
| Production cost | 8% |
| Risk | 7% |

Score out of 100 = sum of (score / 10 x weight x 100). AToure is a B2B
service, so business value carries as much weight as reach. Adjust weights
for a goal (for example raise reach during an event launch), but keep the
dimensions and scale.

## Verdict

| Score | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 70 or more | **Make it.** Put it on the calendar |
| 50 to 69 | **Change the angle.** Give at least 2 specific fixes, then score again |
| Below 50 | **Drop it.** Suggest at least 2 better topics |

## Rules

- Every score needs a reason in one line. No scores by feel.
- A topic that needs a client's or creator's permission scores 1-3 on risk
  until the permission exists.
- Judge production cost against what AToure can really make this month.
