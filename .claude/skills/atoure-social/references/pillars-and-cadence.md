# Content pillars and how often to post

These are starting points for a B2B agency, not measured benchmarks. Adjust
them with AToure's own results (Windsor.ai for Instagram, BrightBean
analytics once connected).

## Pillars for AToure

| Pillar | Share | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Proof and results | 25% | Outcomes with real, permitted numbers; case studies; how the visibility report works |
| On the ground | 25% | Behind the scenes at events, creators at work, logistics, the team |
| Insight | 20% | The creator economy, sponsorship and events in Africa and the diaspora; myths and lessons |
| People | 15% | Creator spotlights, the founder's view, partners (with permission) |
| Offer and call to action | 15% | The Creator Visibility Offer, trial events, sample report requests |

Promotion stays at or under 15 to 20%. Every pillar appears at least once a
week when posting is regular.

**Adjust for the month's goal:**

| Goal | Change |
|---|---|
| Win new clients | +10% proof, +5% offer, -10% people, -5% on the ground |
| Event season | +15% on the ground, -5% each from insight, people and offer |
| Build authority | +10% insight, +5% proof, -10% offer, -5% people |
| Recruit creators | +10% people, +5% on the ground, -10% proof, -5% insight |

## Starting cadence

| Channel | Starting out | Established | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram feed (posts, carousels, reels) | 3 a week | 4 to 5 a week | Reels and carousels carry reach; daily stories on event days |
| LinkedIn company page | 2 a week | 3 a week | Proof and insight lead |
| Founder's LinkedIn profile | 2 a week | 3 to 4 a week | Personal view; usually out-reaches the company page |
| Website | 1 article or case study a month | 2 a month, plus one per event | Feeds LinkedIn and Instagram pieces |

Starting out: get the routine working and measure. Later: double down on
formats that work, run series, and repurpose every pillar piece.

## Building a calendar

1. Pick the month's goal and set the pillar mix from the tables above.
2. Mark fixed dates: events, client announcements (with permission),
   industry dates.
3. Place pillar pieces first, then derived and micro pieces around them
   (`repurposing.md`).
4. Score uncertain ideas with `topic-scoring.md`.
5. Output a week-by-week table: date, channel, pillar, format, working title,
   status. Load it into BrightBean once connected.
