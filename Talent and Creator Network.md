# Talent and Creator Network

AToure describes its roster as a network rather than a list: creators it has worked with directly, who trust AToure to run operations, source brand deals and execute campaigns on their behalf in new markets. The public site organises the network by role (Creators, Artists, Presenters) and by audience tier (Mega, 10 million plus; Macro, 1 to 10 million; Mid, 100 thousand to 1 million; Micro, under 100 thousand), though the individual roster entries themselves were not available to extract from the source pages at the time this file was written.

## Race Weekend Abidjan creator roster

For the Race Weekend event, AToure booked a roster of seven Ivorian creators specifically for the activation, spanning national television faces and automotive and lifestyle voices. Their combined following was reported as close to 3.9 million, summed across accounts rather than deduplicated, so that figure is total audience rather than unique reach. See [Race Weekend Abidjan](Case%20Studies/Race%20Weekend%20Abidjan.md) for the full roster and the follower counts behind each name.

## A note on named but unconfirmed talent

Some proposal documents name potential participants purely as illustrations of the calibre AToure could bring into a project. For example, the Taraji P Henson proposal names several figures in Ivorian cinema (Akissi Delta, Naky Sy Savané, Philippe Lacôte, Michel Gohou, Diêm Camille, Fat Touré) explicitly to show the depth of the local film community. None of those individuals had been approached and none was a confirmed participant at the time the proposal was written. Do not treat names in a proposal document as confirmed relationships unless the case study for a delivered mandate says otherwise.
