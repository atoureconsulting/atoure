# Post-mortem: why it worked, why it flopped

Two modes. Pull figures from Windsor.ai (Instagram, `atoure.management`) or
BrightBean analytics when available. Without figures, say so and keep the
analysis to structure only.

## A. One post

Inputs: the post (text, visuals, link), channel, figures (views or
impressions, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time, clicks), time
posted, and the account's recent average for comparison.

Output:

1. **Verdict:** hit, average or flop, against the account's own average.
   One line on the main reason.
2. **Scores (1 to 10) with reasons:**
   - Hook (first line or first 3 seconds)
   - Structure and pace
   - Value per second or per slide
   - Call to action and engagement prompts
   - Visual quality and brand consistency
   - Channel fit (format, length, hashtags, captions)
3. **Not scored, but discussed:** the topic (evergreen, timely, niche) and
   timing (day and hour, any event it rode).
4. **Three fixes,** most important first, each specific enough to apply to
   the next post.
5. **Data notes:** where the numbers came from and how reliable they are.

Scale: 1-3 a weakness that dragged the post down; 4-6 acceptable; 7-8 better
than usual and worth repeating; 9-10 the reason it worked.

## B. Patterns across posts

Inputs: at least 5 posts with channel and core figures, the date range, and
what counts as a hit (for example saves above the average by half).

Output:

1. **Overview:** number of posts, range, hit rate.
2. **What hits share and flops share:** hook type, topic, structure, time
   posted, length, hashtags, visual style.
3. **Formulas:** two or three combinations that worked
   ("[topic type] + [hook type] + [format]"), each with when it applies, and
   one combination to avoid.
4. **Next steps:** three things to use in the next post; one change for the
   next two weeks.
5. **Limits:** sample size, missing data, platform changes.

Feed what you learn back into the pillar mix and topic scores.
