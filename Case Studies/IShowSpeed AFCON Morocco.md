# IShowSpeed, AFCON Final, Morocco

AFCON 2025 Final. Partners: Confederation of African Football (CAF) and the Royal Moroccan Football Federation (FRMF). Creator: IShowSpeed. Market: Morocco.

## The brief

CAF and the Royal Moroccan Football Federation wanted to integrate a global creator into the AFCON 2025 Final to amplify the tournament's reach into Gen Z audiences globally, and needed a partner who could execute it at the highest level. They came to AToure.

## What AToure did

1. **The mascot concept**: AToure conceived the idea to appoint IShowSpeed as the official AFCON mascot, "Assad." CAF and the Royal Moroccan Football Federation reviewed and signed off on it, but the concept originated with AToure.
2. **State level access**: secured state sponsored visas, official border clearances and private jet clearances for the full team, with VIP pickup from the private terminal in Rabat.
3. **Permits and equipment**: obtained all filming, equipment and drone access permits across Morocco.
4. **Security**: arranged both private security and state security mobilisation for the entire tour, from Casablanca to the AFCON Final in Rabat.
5. **Accommodation**: placed the full team at the top hotel in Rabat and managed all hospitality, transport and logistics from arrival through the Final.
6. **The Final**: secured IShowSpeed's entry to the AFCON Final and coordinated the live mascot reveal.

## The result

The stadium reaction to Speed's reveal as Assad the lion went viral across Africa and globally. AToure's case study attributes a record 6.1 billion digital impressions and 5.2 billion video views for the tournament to the moment, describing these as numbers not previously seen in AFCON history.
