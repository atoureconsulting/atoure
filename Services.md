# Services

AToure organises its work into four service lines, all delivered from one desk so a client does not hand off between vendors as a project moves from strategy to delivery.

## 1. Creator Partnerships

Identifying and securing the right creator for a campaign, based on audience geography, cultural alignment and content fit rather than follower counts alone. Covers:

- African and diaspora creator identification
- Commercial deal structuring
- Brief translation and deliverable definition
- Content compliance and oversight

## 2. Market Activation

For a brand or creator launching in an African or diaspora market, AToure provides the access, infrastructure and local intelligence to land properly rather than just show up. Covers:

- Market entry strategy
- Government and institutional facilitation
- Local partnership identification
- On the ground activation management

## 3. On the Ground Production

The operational layer that makes an international campaign actually happen in market. Covers:

- Visa and permit acquisition
- Local production and crew coordination
- Security, transport and hospitality
- Local vendor and payment management

## 4. African Market Strategy

Advisory work for brands and creators on how to show up authentically in African markets. AToure frames cultural fluency as the foundation of a campaign's success rather than an added feature. Covers:

- Cultural alignment and market advisory
- Creator strategy and audience mapping
- Campaign framing for African audiences
- Post campaign analysis and insights

## The access advantage

AToure's positioning across all four lines is that it is already established in the markets a client is trying to reach, rather than building relationships after a contract is signed. As stated publicly: 12 or more active markets across Africa, Europe and the Middle East, three continents run from one desk, and more than 7 billion views generated across AToure's campaigns to date. These are the company's own published figures and should be treated as AToure's stated claims rather than independently reverified numbers.
