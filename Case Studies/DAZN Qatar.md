# DAZN, Qatar

Brand: DAZN. Market: Qatar. Delivery: full, end to end.

## The brief

DAZN engaged AToure to place a creator in Qatar as the exclusive live broadcaster of a fight, SLIM vs GIB, on a Thursday 28th November. The objective was to drive live stream viewership and pay per view sales through authentic creator content across Instagram, Snapchat and TikTok, with full DAZN brand messaging throughout.

## Content deliverables

- Pre event content: stories, polls, questions and answers, countdowns
- Fight night posts across Instagram, Snapchat and feed posts
- A live stream from the arena, with ringside access and behind the scenes content
- Dressing room access and exclusive fighter interviews
- Tracked links directing the audience to DAZN's own site for pay per view purchase

## What AToure did

1. **Talent placement**: identified and placed the right creator for DAZN's Qatar fight night, matching audience, brand tone and platform strategy.
2. **In country operations**: managed all logistics in Qatar: transport, accommodation, access credentials, ringside positioning and dressing room coordination.
3. **Brand compliance**: ensured all content carried DAZN messaging, tracked links and correct tags across every platform.

## Key outcomes

- DAZN Qatar delivered end to end
- Live ringside content produced on fight night
- Full creator integration within DAZN's broadcast
- Pay per view tracked links across all content
