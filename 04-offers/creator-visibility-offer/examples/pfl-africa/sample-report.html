10Recommendations for the next event

  1. Make the ticket link a required element in every brief. One YouTube video missed it.
  2. Add a replay link to the call to action. Replay was the most asked question.
  3. Food and lifestyle creators beat fight creators on views. Keep one in every market.
  4. Kenya and Uganda gave 25% of views with no local creator. A Nairobi creator would add reach cheaply.
