# How We Write

The hard rules are in `CLAUDE.md`. This file covers the habits, taken from
copy the client wrote.

## Habits

- **Short declarative fragments in capability lists.** "Local creator
  collaborations. Deep access to the fitness community." No verbs needed.
- **State the numbers plainly, then undercut them.** "But the numbers never
  tell the full story," followed by the human result.
- **Plain, confident, specific.** Name the country, the institution, the
  number. No hype words.
- **British spelling**, as the existing files use: colour, organise,
  programme.

## Phrases that sound like AI

Taken from the outreach rules in the CRM. Never use:

- "I'd be happy to", "It's important to note", "It's worth noting"
- "leverage" or "utilize" (write "use")
- "game changer", "cutting edge", "revolutionary", "groundbreaking"
- "seamless", "robust", "streamline", "elevate", "empower"
- "dive deep", "unlock the power of", "transform your"
- "take your X to the next level", "look no further"
- "at the end of the day", "let's be real", "here's the thing"
- "the reality is", "moving forward", "rest assured", "don't miss out"
- "without further ado", "that being said", "in conclusion"

And avoid the rhythms that give it away:

- Three short punchy sentences followed by a medium one.
- More than one rhetorical question in a row.
- Two ideas in one sentence. Split it.

Be specific. "We placed a creator with DAZN for fight night in Qatar" beats
"we have broadcast experience". Lead with what the reader gets, not what
AToure does.

## In pitches

- Congratulate first, specifically.
- Credit what already works before saying anything about the next stage.
- Frame the opportunity as visibility, not as a fault.
- Concede where we were wrong. Never argue a client's own numbers.

## Language

Contacts write in English and French. Reply in the language they use. The
company name stays "AToure Management & Consulting" in every language. In
French copy, never "agence" and never "Conseil".
