# Carousels (Instagram and LinkedIn)

## Sizes

- Instagram: 1080x1350 (4:5) for feed carousels; up to 20 slides, but 7 to
  10 is the working range.
- LinkedIn: a document post (PDF) of 1080x1350 slides, 6 to 12 slides.
- Make the images with Design Engine (`node scripts/export-carousel.js`) or
  Canva, using `brand.config.json`.

## Cover slide (slide 1)

Must stop the scroll in one second.

- 8 words or fewer, large and bold, readable at thumbnail size.
- Creates a curiosity gap or a felt problem.
- No logo on the cover; it weakens curiosity. The handle goes on the last
  slide.
- Formulas: stop doing X ("Stop paying creators for appearances"), a numbered
  list ("5 numbers every sponsor asks for"), before and after, a pain stated
  as fact ("Why creator spend disappears after the event"), a bold claim, an
  identity call ("For anyone running events in Africa").

## Slide arc

| Slides | Role | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | See above |
| 2-3 | Problem | Set up the pain or question |
| 4-7 | Value | One point per slide, 20 words or fewer on the slide |
| 8-9 | Payoff | The key insight or the change |
| Last | Call to action | Save, share, comment or message, matched to the content |

- One idea per slide. If it needs a comma, split it.
- Every slide gives a reason to swipe to the next one.
- Same background, fonts and colours throughout.

## Calls to action

| Type | When | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Save | Practical how-to | "Save this for your next event brief" |
| Share | Useful to a colleague | "Send this to your sponsorship lead" |
| Comment | Opinion or experience | "Which of these does your event track?" |
| Message | Lead | "Message us 'REPORT' for a sample visibility report" |

Never "check out our page" as the main call to action.

## Caption

- First line: a hook that works without the slides.
- Body: expand one slide with a point of view the slides did not include.
- End: echo the last slide's call to action.
- Instagram: 3 to 5 specific hashtags. LinkedIn: 0 to 3.

## Score before handing over (each 1 to 10, weighted)

| Area | Weight |
|---|---|
| Hook strength | 25% |
| Swipe momentum | 20% |
| Value per slide | 20% |
| Call to action | 15% |
| Visual consistency | 10% |
| Caption | 10% |

Pass at 7.0. Below that, fix the weakest area and score again.

## Output

Slide by slide: slide number, on-slide text, visual description, why the
reader swipes on. Then the caption and production notes.
