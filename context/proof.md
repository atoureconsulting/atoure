## Rules for figures

- Views and reach are different numbers. Reach is always lower: views count
  replays, reach counts unique accounts.
- Reach, impressions and media value are estimated, and say so.
- A figure from a client's own campaign belongs to the client. Credit it,
  never claim it.
- Sample reports are fictional. Never quote a sample report figure as real.
