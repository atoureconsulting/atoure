# Rotimi, UK Tour

Case study category: Artist Management. Artist: Rotimi. Work: location scouting and VIP experiences. Market: London, UK.

## The brief

Rotimi's UK tour required more than logistics: it needed AToure's network to unlock the city at the level his profile demands. This meant premium shoot locations, VIP access to London's top hospitality venues, and full operational support for Rotimi and his team throughout the tour.

## What AToure delivered

- Shoot location sourcing across premium London locations
- Full media production support for the UK tour
- VIP experiences at top London hospitality sites

## What AToure did

1. **Location scouting**: scouted and secured premium London shoot locations, giving Rotimi and his team high quality media content for the UK tour.
2. **VIP access**: arranged exclusive access to London's top hospitality venues, matching Rotimi's global profile.
3. **Full operations**: managed all ground logistics, transport, accommodation, scheduling and full production support throughout the tour.

## Key outcomes

- Premium London shoot locations secured
- VIP access at top London hospitality venues
- High quality media content produced across the tour

No public view or reach figures are attached to this case study; it is presented on operational and access delivery rather than reported campaign performance.
