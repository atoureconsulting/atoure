# IShowSpeed, Ivory Coast

2024. Brands: MTN, Solibra, Sublime (referred to elsewhere on the same page as Snedai). Creator: IShowSpeed. Market: Ivory Coast.

## The brief

IShowSpeed's Ivory Coast visit required building an entire operational and commercial infrastructure from zero: local sponsors, state level facilitation, security forces, cultural programming and logistics, for one of the world's biggest creators landing in West Africa with no existing local network. AToure handled every permit, every visa, every vehicle and every security operative, plus the cultural moment that went viral around the world.

## What AToure delivered

- 15 state visas secured for the full touring team
- Equipment and filming permits across all jurisdictions
- 600 or more security personnel mobilised city wide
- Restricted zone access and privatisation of public spaces
- 3 local sponsors sourced and closed from scratch: MTN, Solibra and Snedai
- Presidential recognition for the activation

## What AToure did

1. **Sponsors**: identified, negotiated and closed MTN, Solibra and Snedai as official local sponsors, from zero pre existing relationships.
2. **Visas and entry**: secured 15 state sponsored visas and official border clearances for the full touring team.
3. **Permits**: obtained all equipment, filming and broadcast permits across all Ivory Coast jurisdictions.
4. **Security**: mobilised 600 or more security personnel city wide to manage and control mass public gatherings throughout the tour.
5. **Cultural moments**: mobilised Miss Ivory Coast, Magic System and Afro B, and coordinated the Zaouli dance activation.
6. **Full logistics**: all ground transport, accommodation, hospitality, drone deployment and live stream production, handled by AToure on the ground.

## The result

IShowSpeed's participation in the Zaouli cultural dance became one of the most shared creator moments in West African digital history. Baba Touré was personally recognised by the Ivory Coast Minister of Tourism for AToure's contribution to the country's global cultural profile.

## Key outcomes

- 55.8 million Instagram reel views
- 10 million YouTube views
- 3 sponsors placed from scratch (MTN, Solibra, Snedai)
- 15 state visas secured
- 600 or more security personnel mobilised
- Presidential recognition
- Ministerial endorsement from the Ivory Coast Minister of Tourism
- Miss Ivory Coast and Magic System mobilised for the activation

AToure's own framing of this mandate is that it stands as the company's most complete demonstration of ground up operational capability: building a full commercial, logistical and creative infrastructure in a new market with zero pre existing relationships, and delivering a moment that reached the President's office.
