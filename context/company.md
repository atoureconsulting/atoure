# The Company

## In one line

AToure Management & Consulting builds the project and runs it on the ground
as the operator. One team from the first meeting to the last day, with no
handoffs between advisers and operators.

## What we do

Five services, every one run by our own team in market (from the Services
page on the website):

1. **Market entry.** Where to start, who to meet and how to land properly.
2. **Government and institutions.** Ministerial and institutional access,
   permits, visas, clearances, official endorsements.
3. **Operations and logistics.** Security, transport, accommodation, venues,
   crew, local vendors and payments.
4. **Communications and media.** Press, broadcasters, creator partnerships,
   campaign delivery and reporting.
5. **Strategic introductions.** Business partners, sponsors, sector
   specialists, private client arrangements.

The offer we are taking to new clients now is the **Creator Visibility
Offer** (`offers/creator-visibility-offer/`): talent access, creator
management on the ground, and the visibility report. Creators produce
visibility. AToure makes that visibility provable, so it can be sold to
sponsors and shown to investors.

## How we work

Listen, plan, open, run. The client tells us the country, the sector and
what they need. We map the people, permissions and partners. We make the
introductions and secure the access. Our team operates on the ground until
the work is delivered.

## What we don't compromise on

- **On the ground.** Our own people in market, not a partner of a partner.
- **Relationships first.** Access built over years of trust. We protect it
  by delivering.
- **Discretion.** Many clients never appear in case studies. Every brief,
  contact and figure is confidential.
