# One idea, three channels: Instagram, LinkedIn, website

## The content pyramid

| Layer | What it is | AToure examples |
|---|---|---|
| Pillar | Deep, long piece | Website case study or article, a 3 to 8 minute LinkedIn or website video, a webinar or talk |
| Derived | Medium pieces on one sub-topic | LinkedIn post, Instagram carousel, 60-second reel |
| Micro | One sharp point | Instagram story, quote card, LinkedIn one-liner, a single stat graphic |

One pillar piece should yield roughly 8 to 15 derived and micro pieces.

## Method

1. **Extract the core:** the thesis in one sentence, 3 to 5 key points,
   lines worth quoting, and real data points (permitted only).
2. **Plan the pieces:** list each piece with channel, format and which key
   point it carries.
3. **Write each piece native to its channel:** it should read as if it was
   written for that channel, not cut down from something else. Each piece
   stands on its own without the original.
4. **Keep one message:** every piece carries the same thesis; none
   contradicts another.
5. **Sequence:** pillar first, derived pieces from the next day, micro pieces
   in between. Schedule in BrightBean once connected.

## Recipes

**Website article or case study (pillar)**
- Title that states the result or the question. Short intro: why it matters.
- Sections with plain headings; real numbers labelled with their source;
  a closing call to action (contact, sample report).
- Check the page with Impeccable before it goes live.

**LinkedIn post**
- Hook in the first one or two lines.
- 150 to 300 words, short paragraphs, one idea.
- A business outcome or lesson; ends with a question or a clear next step.
- Link to the website article in the first comment or at the end.

**LinkedIn document carousel**
- 6 to 12 slides of 1080x1350, one point each, cover question or claim, last
  slide summary and call to action. See `carousels.md`.

**Instagram carousel**
- 7 to 10 slides, visual first, 20 words or fewer per slide. See
  `carousels.md`.

**Instagram reel (30 to 60 s)**
- Hook in the first 3 seconds, three points, call to action. See `video.md`.

**Instagram story set**
- 3 to 6 frames: hook, one stat or behind-the-scenes clip, a poll or question
  sticker, a link to the post or website.

**Quote card**
- One line from the pillar, under 20 words, with attribution, in brand
  colours.

## Output

1. A plan: source type, thesis, key points, quotable lines, data points,
   list of pieces by channel.
2. Each piece written in full.
3. A suggested publishing order.
