# Chicken Shop Date

Case study category: Brand Collaboration. Brand: Chicken Shop. Talent: Manlikeisaac. Market: UK.

## The brief

Chicken Shop partnered with AToure to promote their "Seriously Good" Chicken and the launch of their new Honey Soy Wings. The goal was to engage a Gen Z audience and make Chicken Shop trend across Instagram and TikTok through Manlikeisaac, integrating the brand into his short form content and his 4 million subscriber YouTube activation.

AToure's own case study states that Manlikeisaac's video reached nearly double the industry standard engagement rate, sparking awareness around the Honey Soy Wings launch.

## What AToure did

1. **Talent placement**: placed Manlikeisaac with Chicken Shop to reach two key demographics at the core of his following, the halal audience and London's Gen Z youth.
2. **Abdu Rozik collaboration**: brokered a collaboration with Abdu Rozik, a major talent based in Dubai with a large following across the Middle East, amplifying the campaign's reach.
3. **Full delivery**: managed the full campaign, production coordination, brand briefing, content compliance and delivery.

## Campaign stats

- 1 million Instagram views reached
- 1.2 million TikTok community
- 200 thousand YouTube views activated
- 2.4 million total campaign views
- 30 thousand or more total engagements across platforms

## Key outcomes

- 2.4 million total campaign views
- Engagement rate roughly double the industry standard, per AToure's own framing
- Abdu Rozik collaboration closed
- Halal and Gen Z demographics both reached
- Honey Soy Wings product launch amplified

AToure's own framing: by pairing Manlikeisaac with Chicken Shop and bringing in Abdu Rozik from the Middle East, the campaign resonated authentically across two distinct audiences.
