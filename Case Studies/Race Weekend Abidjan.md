# Race Weekend, Abidjan

6 and 7 June 2026. Two days at Espace Superkart, Abidjan, Ivory Coast. Described by AToure as the first premium Formula 1 activation staged in Cote d'Ivoire.

## The concept

Formula 1 has a fanbase in Abidjan, but nowhere to watch it properly together. Race Weekend built that room: a live projection of the Monaco Grand Prix on a giant screen, with a karting circuit running alongside it, a supercar paddock, a VIP lounge above the track, and 300 guests a day drawn from the city's young professional and motorsport communities.

## The format

**Day one, Saturday 6 June**: premium welcome and sponsor wall, karting sessions on the Superkart circuit, supercar paddock and photo positions, signature cocktails and a DJ set, networking across the venue.

**Day two, Sunday 7 June**: the live Monaco Grand Prix on the giant screen, hosted segments and floor interviews, the VIP lounge and coffee experience above the track, guest driver demonstrations, prize giving and close.

## Guest of honour

Omar Jahidi, Formula 4 driver and Formula 3 test driver, winner of the Aramco F4 Abu Dhabi. Present across both days for demonstrations, exclusive content and direct exchanges with guests.

## Other guests and amplification figures

- Sindou Fofana, President of FISA, the Ivorian motorsport federation
- Gary Chaynes and Carole Roinet, rally champions, Cote d'Ivoire
- Maja Zuber, Formula 1 marketing specialist
- Willy Dumbo, host and animator across both days

**Event and operations**: concept, positioning and the two day programme design; venue partnership and site build at Espace Superkart; live broadcast setup and giant screen projection; karting, supercar paddock and on track programming; VIP lounge, hospitality and guest management.
