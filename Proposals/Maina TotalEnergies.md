# Abdullahi Maina x TotalEnergies

Creator proposition, 2025, prepared for TotalEnergies and Le Média Positif Afrique, for a Clean Cooking Africa documentary brief. This is a creator pitch, not a delivered mandate.

## The creator

Abdullahi Maina, a bilingual (English and French) food creator with Nigerian roots, positioned by AToure as the right host for a documentary about how access to clean cooking energy changes life for African households. He runs two accounts: @itsyaboymaina, a mainstream lifestyle and entertainment channel, and @mainavsfood, a dedicated food channel with an African food playlist and a "Cooking with Maina" series.

## Why he fits the brief, per the proposal

The brief calls for a documentary host who can carry the clean cooking story with authenticity, curiosity and reach. The proposal argues Maina already lives at the intersection of food and Africa rather than needing to be briefed into it, that his bilingual capability opens both Francophone and Anglophone Sub Saharan African markets without a translator on screen, and that his two account structure gives the campaign both a mainstream reach engine and a dedicated food credibility anchor.

## Proposed structure across the documentary's three acts

1. **Le Constat** (the reality of charcoal and wood cooking): Maina visits families and cooks alongside them on traditional stoves.
2. **Les Solutions** (the shift to LPG, TotalEnergies stations and pay as you cook technology): Maina explores the infrastructure with a food creator's natural curiosity.
3. **L'Impact** (testimonials and the 2030 ambition of 100 million beneficiaries): Maina closes by cooking the same dish before and after, framed as sensory and emotional.

## Proposed deliverables

A roughly 30 minute YouTube documentary hosted by Maina across all three acts, bilingual field sequences, cross promotion to @itsyaboymaina on release, plus short form TikTok cuts from both @mainavsfood and @itsyaboymaina and Instagram Reels.
