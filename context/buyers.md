# Who Buys

The current goal is new Creator Visibility clients outside combat sports.
These are the buyers to go after. The full table, with creators that fit and
extra report metrics, is in
`offers/creator-visibility-offer/adapting-by-event-type.md`.

## What every buyer has in common

They run a live event or campaign that already uses creators, or could. They
need to show sponsors, investors, a board or a ministry what that visibility
was worth, and today they cannot. The proof is the product.

## Target sectors

The first three are the ones the offer already names as examples to build
next. Baba sets the final priority.

| Sector | Who signs | What they need to prove | Lead with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Music festival | Promoter, partnerships lead | Reach beyond ticket holders; sponsor activation value | Sponsor activation, diaspora reach |
| Conference or trade fair | Organiser, government body | International visibility; delegates for the next edition | Country reach, speaker clips |
| Tourism campaign | Ministry, tourism board | Global awareness of the destination | International and diaspora split |
| Concert or tour date | Promoter, artist management | Buzz in each city; ticket sell through | Ticket clicks per creator |
| Brand launch | Brand marketing team | Product visibility and conversions | Promo codes, product on screen |
| Cultural event or carnival | Organiser, city, ministry of culture | Cultural export, tourism pull | International audience share |

Combat sports (PFL Africa, African MMA League) are where the offer started.
They stay open but are not the focus of new prospecting.

## What makes a good prospect

- An event three to six months out, in or near a market where we operate on
  the ground.
- A commercial reason to care now: a new sponsor cycle, an investor round,
  an expansion, a new edition to sell.
- Already uses creators, or has a sponsor who would want creator proof.
- A named person we can reach, ideally through an existing relationship.

## What makes a poor one

- No live moment and no creators: the offer has nothing to measure.
- Wants paid ads or a campaign bought on its behalf. That is not what we
  sell.

## Lessons so far

- Clients may already run creators well. PFL Africa did. Research before
  assuming a gap.
- The report is the part clients valued. Offer it on its own when management
  is a no.
- Talent access is a real way in, even unpaid at first.
