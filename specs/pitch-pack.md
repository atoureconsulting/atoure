# Spec: Pitch Pack

Made by: Business development, after Baba approves a prospect brief.
Saved in: `customers/<prospect>/`.

Everything Baba needs to send a pitch. Drafts only: Baba sends.

## Inputs

- The approved `brief.md`.
- `offers/creator-visibility-offer/pitch-playbook.md` for the email order
  and the lessons from the first pitches.
- `offers/creator-visibility-offer/adapting-by-event-type.md` for the deck.
- The closest demo in `demos/`.
- `specs/client-design.md` for the look.

## What is in the pack

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `email.md` | The pitch email with a subject line, in the order the pitch playbook sets out |
| `whatsapp.md` | The short follow up: email sent, the two parts in one line, ask for a call |
| `design.md` | The client's look and the merged palette (`specs/client-design.md`) |
| `deck-source/` | The eight slide deck, copied from the closest demo, adapted, and restyled to the merged design |
| `sample-report.html` | Only if a demo exists for this sector. Marked fictional |
| `notes.md` | What changed from the template, what is still a guess, questions for Baba |

## Done means

- The report appears in the first half of the email.
- Slide 2 uses public facts, with sources in the speaker notes.
- Every attachment named in the email exists in the folder.
- Nothing quotes a price. Where the pitch touches cost, it says the work is
  scoped to the client's budget and responsibilities, and `notes.md` lists
  what Baba needs to price it: budget, scope, number of events, markets and
  creators.
- It passes `REVIEW.md`.
