# Spec: Prospect Brief

Made by: Business development.
Saved as: `customers/<prospect>/brief.md`.

One page that tells Baba whether a prospect is worth pitching, and how.

## Inputs

- The prospect and the event, from the weekly routine or from Baba.
- Public sources: their site, social accounts, press, event listings.
- `context/buyers.md` and
  `offers/creator-visibility-offer/adapting-by-event-type.md`.

## The brief

1. **Who they are.** Organisation, event, date, city, sector.
2. **Who signs.** The named decision maker and their role, and how we could
   reach them. Say if there is an existing relationship.
3. **What already works.** Editions, growth, sponsors, broadcast, how they
   use creators today. Every fact with its source.
4. **Why now.** The commercial reason: sponsor cycle, investor round,
   expansion, a new edition to sell.
5. **The fit.** Which event type row applies, what they would need to prove,
   which creators fit, which extra report metrics.
6. **Way in.** Trial, report only, or talent access, and why.
7. **Risks.** What we do not know. What could make this a poor fit.
8. **Recommendation.** Pitch now, watch, or drop. One sentence.
9. **Sources.** Every link used.

## Done means

- It fits on one page.
- Every claim about the prospect has a source.
- No gap is stated as fact.
- It passes `REVIEW.md`.
