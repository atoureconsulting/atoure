# Video: ideas, scripts, covers

## 1. Generating ideas

Start from what AToure actually does and has access to. Produce 8 to 12
ideas, each with: working title, format, length, channel, production route,
what footage or permission it needs. Then score them with
`topic-scoring.md` and keep the top 3 to 5.

Idea families that fit AToure:

| Family | Example ideas | Best channel | Production route |
|---|---|---|---|
| On the ground at events | "48 hours running creators at a fight night", "What happens backstage before a creator posts" | Instagram reels, stories | Phone footage by the team on the day (permission from creators and venue) |
| Proof and method | "How we turn creator posts into numbers a sponsor can buy", "Views are not visibility" | LinkedIn video, Instagram reel | Lemo-Opuscar: Data Storytelling (`dataviz`), Whiteboard (`whiteboard`), Sketchnote (`sketchnote`) |
| The audience map | "Where an African event's audience really watches from: Africa and the diaspora" | Both | Lemo-Opuscar: 3D Map Explainer (`map-explainer`) with real, permitted data |
| Myth busting | "Three things event organisers get wrong about creators" | Both | Founder talking to camera, 45 to 90 seconds |
| Creator spotlight | "Meet the creators we work with in Kigali" | Instagram | Creator-supplied clips with permission, or a short interview |
| Countdown and hype | "7 days to Race Weekend Abidjan" series | Instagram stories and reels | Lemo-Opuscar: Live Countdown (`countdown`), or Higgsfield |
| Recap as news | "The week in creator-driven sport" | Instagram, LinkedIn | Lemo-Opuscar: Broadcast News Package (`news-package`) or Caption News (`caption-news`) |
| Sponsor point of view | "What a sponsor sees in a visibility report" | LinkedIn | Screen recording of the sample report, labelled SAMPLE on screen |
| Fight or match breakdown | Annotated replay of a key moment | Instagram | Lemo-Opuscar: Sports Telestrator (`telestrator`); needs the rights holder's permission for the footage |

Use real trends: check Higgsfield `tiktok_music_trending` for current audio,
and Windsor.ai for which AToure posts already performed best.

## 2. Short video script (60 seconds or less)

**Three hooks, scored.** Write three hooks from different types: curiosity
gap, pattern break, identity call ("If you run events in Africa..."), pain
point, authority. Score each 1 to 5 on curiosity, surprise, who-it's-for
clarity and clarity in 3 seconds (max 20). Use the highest.

**Structure by length:**

| Length | Structure |
|---|---|
| 7 to 15 s | Hook 0-2s, one point 2-10s, close 10-15s |
| 15 to 30 s | Hook 0-3s, problem 3-8s, answer 8-20s, call to action 20-30s |
| 30 to 60 s | Hook 0-3s, problem 3-8s, answer 8-40s, insight and call to action 40-60s |

**Script format:**

```
[0-3s] HOOK
Voice: "..."
On screen: "..."
Shot: ...

[3-8s] PROBLEM
Voice: "..."
Shot: ...
```

Spoken English runs at about 150 words a minute: 30 seconds is about 75
words. Short spoken sentences, with notes in brackets for pauses and
emphasis. Change the picture every 2 to 3 seconds in short video.

**Caption:** opens with a different version of the hook, adds one thing the
video did not say, ends with one call to action. 3 to 5 specific hashtags,
no generic ones.

**Cover:** 3 to 5 words, large, readable at thumbnail size; a face or one
strong object; brand colours. Make it with Design Engine
(`instagram_story_reel_cover` 1080x1920) or Higgsfield.

**Score before handing over (out of 100):**

| Area | Weight | Checks |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 25 | Curiosity, surprise, clear in 3 seconds |
| Content | 25 | Value per second, clear problem and answer |
| Caption and call to action | 20 | Adds to the video, one clear action |
| Format | 15 | 9:16, captions inside safe areas, right length, cover |
| Shareability | 15 | Something worth saving or sending to a colleague |

Hand over at 80 or more; below 60, rewrite and score again.

## 3. Longer video (over 60 seconds: LinkedIn, website, YouTube)

- **Hook in three parts:** grab (0-5s, the single strongest line, no intro),
  promise (5-15s, what the viewer gets), stakes (15-30s, what they miss by
  leaving).
- **Sections:** each one is pattern break, content, one-line recap, a reason
  to keep watching.
- **Pattern breaks** every 60 to 90 seconds: new shot, graphic, sound, or a
  number on screen.
- **Calls to action:** a soft one about a quarter of the way in, a "the most
  important part is next" pull at about 60%, a clear ask at the end. Never
  end on "thanks for watching".
- Mark at least three places viewers are likely to drop off, with a fix for
  each.

## 4. Covers and thumbnails

- One idea per image. Never more than 5 words.
- Readable at the size of a phone thumbnail: check by shrinking the image.
- Real people beat illustrations where you have permission.
- Do not let AI generators write the words: add text in Design Engine or
  Canva so spelling and fonts are exact.
- Check claims on the cover are true and permitted.
